About ATS Resume Score

ATS Resume Score is a free online tool designed to help job seekers understand how well their resume aligns with a specific job description.

Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes before a human ever sees them. Our tool analyzes keyword relevance, role alignment, and overall resume compatibility to give you a clear ATS score.

ATS Resume Score runs entirely in your browser. Your resume files are not uploaded to any server or stored by us.

This tool is intended for educational and informational purposes only and does not guarantee interview calls or job offers.
